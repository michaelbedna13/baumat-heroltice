Accommodation

Prices are per bed per night for stays of two nights or more. A one-night stay costs more.

Flat-rate fees and the local tourist tax are added to the accommodation price, see Fees and hire. Detailed facilities for each type are listed under Accommodation.

  • Apartment

    3 separate rooms with two beds each, private shower and toilet, separate kitchen.

    290 CZKbed / night, 1 night 380 CZK

  • Cottages

    4 cottages with three beds each, bathroom facilities right next door, veranda with seating.

    220 CZKbed / night, 1 night 280 CZK

  • Cabins

    Cabins for 3 to 4 people, simple furnishings, no power sockets.

    180 CZKbed / night, 1 night 220 CZK

  • Cabins by the Main Gate

    Rooms with two beds, a desk, a reading lamp and power sockets.

    180 CZKbed / night, 1 night 220 CZK

Whole resort

The resort just for your group, with no other guests. For weddings, celebrations and corporate events.

  • Whole resort rental

    Complete privacy and free use of all facilities and activities for your group.

    40,000 CZKper weekend

  • Deep cleaning

    Cleaning of the whole resort so it's ready for the next guests.

    8,000 CZK

  • Cleaning of the main kitchen and dining hall

    Cleaning of work surfaces, appliances and the dining area.

    4,000 CZK

Space and equipment rental

We don't provide catering, but you can rent the kitchen, dining hall and clubhouse.

The kitchenette price depends on the size of your group: if the group occupies more than two cabins, the first kitchenette costs 300 CZK per day and each additional one 100 CZK per day. Charged per cabin or other accommodation unit.

  • Kitchen

    Professional equipment for camps, weddings, corporate and sports events: combi oven, commercial dishwasher, gas cooker, large electric frying pan, food processor, two fridges and two freezers.

    900 CZKper day + energy

  • Dining hall

    Space for catering for up to 100 guests.

    800 CZKper day + energy

  • Kitchenette

    A smaller kitchen with a cooker, a fridge and a table for up to 15 people.

    100 to 300 CZKper day

  • Clubhouse

    Seating with a fireplace, billiards, a table tennis table and board games.

    800 CZKper day + energy

Fees and hire

Flat-rate fees apply to every guest. Hire charges apply only to what you actually borrow.

When renting the whole resort, energy is charged by actual consumption (electricity per kWh, gas per m³) at the suppliers' current rates. Otherwise, a flat fee of 50 CZK per person per day applies.

  • Flat-rate fees

    • Linen fee: 100 CZK per person
    • Visitor fee: 100 CZK per person
    • Energy: 50 CZK per person per day

    from 100 CZK

  • Hire

    • Tennis court: 250 CZK per hour (non-staying guests only)
    • Volleyball court: 300 CZK per hour
    • Billiards: 100 CZK per hour
    • Boats: 200 CZK per hour
    • Beer tap equipment: 250 CZK per day
    • Portable round grill or large square grill: 150 CZK per day
    • Fridge in the cottages: 60 CZK per day
    • Electric heater: 100 CZK per day

    from 60 CZK

  • Local tourist tax

    Accommodation fee under Act No. 128/2000 Coll., in accordance with Act No. 565/1990 Coll. on local fees. Applies all year round.

    10 CZKperson / day

  • Lost key

    Penalty fee for losing an accommodation key.

    1,000 CZK

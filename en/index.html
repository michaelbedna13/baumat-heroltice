For celebrations, weddings, camps and companies

We host small groups as well as large events. The dining hall seats up to 100 guests, and the pool, playground and clubhouse keep everyone busy.

Who comes to stay

Every group needs something different. We'll gladly help you find what suits yours.

  • Family celebrations

    Accommodation, a dining hall for the party and things to do for children and adults.

  • Weddings

    A celebration in nature, a reception in the dining hall for up to 100 guests and accommodation for everyone.

  • Children's camps

    Cabins, a dining hall for up to 100 people, a pool and a playground all in one place.

  • Corporate events

    Team building, workshops or informal get-togethers with colleagues.

What you'll find at the resort

Water and sports during the day, a campfire or the clubhouse with its fireplace in the evening.

  • Swimming pool

    Swimming or relaxing by clear water.

  • Tennis

    Courts and racket hire.

  • Beach volleyball

    We'll gladly lend you a ball.

  • Campfire area

    Roasting sausages and evenings by the fire.

  • Children's playground

    A swing and a slide.

  • Clubhouse

    Fireplace, billiards, table tennis and board games.

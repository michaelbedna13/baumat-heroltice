Accommodation at the resort

From an apartment with its own bathroom to simple cabins close to nature. Choose what suits your group.

Availability Price list 2026

Four types of accommodation

Prices are per bed per night for stays of two nights or more. A one-night stay costs more; see the price list for details.

Price list 2026
  • Apartment6 beds, private bathroom and kitchen290 CZKper bed per night
  • Slušovice units12 beds in 4 units, veranda220 CZKper bed per night

Apartment

290 CZKper bed per night, 380 CZK for a one-night stay

The most comfortable accommodation at the resort. Your own bathroom and kitchen, so you have everything you need.

  • 3 separate rooms, each with two single beds
  • Private bathroom with shower and toilet
  • Separate kitchen with a fridge, microwave and kettle
  • Power sockets and reading lamps in every room
  • Wardrobe in every room
Ask about dates

Slušovice units

220 CZKper bed per night, 280 CZK for a one-night stay

Separate units with a veranda for smaller groups. The bathroom facilities are right next door.

  • 4 separate units, each with three single beds
  • Shower and toilet right next to the rooms
  • Outdoor seating on the verandas
  • Power sockets, lamps, a desk with a chair and a wardrobe
  • Small fridge for an extra charge
Ask about dates

Classic cabins

180 CZKper bed per night, 220 CZK for a one-night stay

Simple accommodation close to nature. The most popular choice for camps and large groups.

  • Cabins 1 to 4 have three single beds
  • Cabins 5 to 24 have two bunk beds, i.e. 4 beds
  • Two clothes lockers, a table and a chair
  • The cabins have no power sockets
  • Shared bathroom facilities in the main building
Ask about dates

Cabins by the main gate

180 CZKper bed per night, 220 CZK for a one-night stay

Quiet twin rooms with a desk and power sockets, suitable for working too.

  • Room with two single beds
  • Wardrobe, desk and chair
  • Reading lamp and plenty of power sockets
  • Shared bathroom facilities in the main building

Facilities for the whole group

We don't provide catering, but you can rent the kitchen and dining hall and cook for yourselves.
